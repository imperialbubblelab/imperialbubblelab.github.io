    Plot hygiene
    
          
      
            
    • Label every axis with the quantity and its units, units in brackets.
      • 
            
    • Lines for continuous or ordered data, points for separate measurements, bars for categories.
      • 
            
    • Tell series apart by line style or marker as well as colour, and add a legend.
      • 
            
    • Use a log scale when values span orders of magnitude, and show uncertainty with error bars.
