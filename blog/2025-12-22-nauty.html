Delightfully NAUTY in 2025

22 Dec 2025

I am delighted to have made it through to the end of my first academic term with a newly blue lanyard. Are you delighted? Or are you suspicious of the word delighted, where etymologically, you are technically being "intensely lured away"[1] from your meat-based sparse attention mechanism. 13th century Europe as it would seem, has foreshadowed the tiktok economy..

The first assault on your attention would relate to a podcast that I am delighted to soon launch, "The AI Interviews"; whereby my friend Paddy and I would talk to people with an interesting opinion on an area of this vast topic. In this era of fragmented attention and ultra-granular cultural clustering, do we need another one of these? To swerve around this question, I would like to delight you to the jingle that I composed for this show, which was, actually quite delightful.

The brief for the jingle was for a show that is curious but grounded in conversation, not too technical yet does not shy away from it. ChatGPT reckons that "It would feel like someone opening a notebook, leaning in, and saying 'okay—walk me through that,' while a tiny hint of “future-tech” winks in the background." For the violin, it suggested some quick staccato notes, following by multi harmony double stops and ending with a resolution. As I type this current paragraph, having no clue what ChatGPT would say before I started this article, am quite amazed that the end result of the jingle did sort of resemble this AI structure. "You're absolutely right", I could almost hear myself say, as I become one with the Cyberman..

The second assault on your attention regards the issue of NAUTY-ness. And by that I of course mean the graph automorphism of a BCC lattice (NAUTY = No AUTomorphism, Yes? - mathematicians are canonically wild). Now, there is a lecture to be give on graph automorphism that relates to another PhD post in the near future, but perhaps in an audio form of interview-interviewee dynamic, let's say occuring somewhere around March in 2026, available on Spotify or Apple podcast!

What is interesting, however, is the premise of NAUTY. And by that I of course refer to the grasping of seemingly infinite new developments with only finite attention, you are of course currently delighted every second of second minute of every day. We become ‘卷’ (pronounced Juǎn, not in the Spanish sense, more inline with Ju(-jitsu)-an), the 2021 Chinese netizen word of the year which observes that the system created by involution (hyper-competition) resembles a vortex, it causes individuals to abandon independent thought and choice, surrendering themselves to their environment. That does not sound good at all. But the more pressing issue is how would we get out of this 卷-state of seemingly local minima?

One solution would be to reduce your phase space by filtering out some duplicates, and then reduce even more by constraints and then even more until it is manageable. And finally, having reduced it to one and then run (or while you run) an hour-long verification to see if it is as you thought it was..
